# Bid comparison: Bid1 vs Bid2

## Summary

Bid1 (Dallas ISD, JA-207652) is a very large, multi-tier, multi-year device program (about 230,000 units across ten line items, 3+1+1 year term) with white-glove services and heavy paperwork. Bid2 (Maryland Treasurer, #E20P4600040) is a small, single-spec buy of 30 Dell Latitude 5550 laptops and 30 docks, delivered within 45 days of award, under an existing master contract. Bid2 is due 06/10/2024 and Bid1 on July 9, 2024, so Bid2 closes about a month earlier. Neither bid states a bid bond requirement. Bid1 suits a bidder with scale, breadth across Chromebooks, tablets and desktops, and services capability. Bid2 suits a Dell-authorized reseller with access to the Maryland master contract.

## Key differences

- **Scope and quantities:** Bid1: 10 line items. Chromebooks 100,000 (50,000 Tier 1 touch, 50,000 Tier 2 non-touch), 5,000 Tier 3 Windows laptops, 50,000 tablets, 10,000 staff laptops, 10,000 desktops, 10,000 monitors. Bid2: only 30 Dell Latitude 5550 laptops and 30 Dell WD22TB4 docks.
- **Due date and submission:** Bid1 is due July 9, 2024 at 2:00 PM CST (Addendum 2 moved it from June 27). Submission is via the iSupplier portal or a sealed envelope to Dallas ISD Procurement Services in Dallas, TX. Bid2 is due 06/10/2024 at 2:00 PM EDT, electronically only via eMMA. Bid1 has a non-mandatory pre-bid meeting on June 10, 2024 at 2:00 PM CST (TEAMS). Bid2 states no pre-bid meeting.
- **Contract vehicle and term:** Bid1 uses Central Texas Purchasing Alliance (CTPA). Its term is 3 years plus two 1-year extensions, up to 5 years total. Bid2 uses the Desktop, Laptop and Tablet 2015 Master Contract 060B5400007. Its term is not stated.
- **Product specificity:** Bid1 gives generic spec tiers ("or equivalent", e.g. Celeron N100, i5/i7, Chrome OS, iOS 17 tablet). Warranties are 1 year minimum for Chromebooks and tablets and 3 years for Windows laptops and desktops. The extracted spec text is truncated at the Tier 2 Staff Laptop. No model or part numbers are stated. Bid2 is brand-specific: Dell Latitude 5550 with Core Ultra 5 125U, 16GB RAM, 256GB SSD, Win 11 Pro and a 3-year extended Dell warranty. It lists about 38 Dell part numbers, and Dell is the manufacturer for registration.
- **Services and delivery:** Bid1 requires white-glove delivery (decaling, asset reporting, etching, delivery to varied locations), asset tagging, Windows Autopilot enrollment and Google Management enrollment. Delivery is by project quotes starting September 2024, on a schedule coordinated with Dallas ISD ED TECH. Bid2 states no installation requirement and requires delivery within 45 days of award.
- **Required documents:** Bid1 requires Form 1295, a signed W-9, MWBE forms, a Certificate of Insurance/Bond within 10 days of award, and SF-LLL if applicable. Bid2 requires a Mercury Affidavit and a Dell/distributor Letter of Authorization upon request. Bid2 therefore needs manufacturer authorization. Bid1 does not state it.
- **Payment terms:** Bid1 does not state payment terms. Bid2 requires invoices within 10 days of equipment delivery.
- **Buyer and contact:** Bid1: Dallas ISD, Jasmine Alzate (JALZATE@dallasisd.org, no phone stated). Bid2: State of Maryland Treasurer's Office, Tamaira Hawkins (thawkins@treasurer.state.md.us, 410-260-7533).

## Similarities

- Neither bid states a bid bond requirement.
- Both require 2:00 PM deadlines with electronic submission available.
- Both involve Windows 11 Pro laptops with warranty of at least 3 years on the comparable Windows models.

## Considerations for a bidder

- Bid2's deadline of 06/10/2024 is the same date as Bid1's pre-bid meeting and about a month before Bid1's due date. Plan resources accordingly.
- Bid1's volume and white-glove and enrollment services raise pricing, logistics and inventory risk. The type of Certificate of Insurance/Bond is unclear.
- Bid1 pricing needs the full spec text, which is truncated after the Tier 2 Staff Laptop in the extracted data. It also needs the CTPA terms.
- Bid2 requires access to Dell and a Dell LOA, and the bidder must be able to use the Maryland master contract 060B5400007. The 45-day delivery window and the 10-day invoicing deadline are firm.
- Bid2's contract term is not stated. Confirm it in the source documents.
