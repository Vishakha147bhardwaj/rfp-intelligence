## Q: Compare the warranty requirements of both bids.

**Answer:** Bid 1 (Dallas ISD RFP JA-207652): Warranty minimums are 1 year for student Chromebooks and 3 years for Windows laptops, staff laptops and display monitors [1][4][7]. Warranties start when equipment is placed into service, must equal or exceed those offered to the general public, and service or repairs must be done at no cost within 5 business days, or a temporary replacement device must be provided [3]. All warranties must be official OEM/manufacturer warranties, with no accidental damage protection required [7][8]. Additional warranty options can be submitted as attachments with extra pricing [7][8]. Bid 2 (Maryland Treasurer's Office Dell laptop PORFP): It requires a Dell Limited Hardware Warranty extended to 3 years for all machines purchased [9]. The warranty runs from the date of delivery to 3 years after delivery, and a warranty certificate or affidavit must be presented upon award [9]. The evidence does not give a repair turnaround time for Bid 2. In short, both bids require 3-year manufacturer warranties on laptops, but Bid 1 adds tiered terms, a 5-business-day repair requirement and an in-service start date, while Bid 2 specifies a Dell extended warranty starting at delivery [3][9].
